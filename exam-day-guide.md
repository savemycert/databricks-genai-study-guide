# Databricks GenAI Exam-Day Guide

This page covers the logistics of the Databricks Certified Generative AI Engineer Associate exam: booking through Kryterion, rescheduling, the online proctored setup, the exam format, scoring, results, retakes and recertification. Every fact below links to the official Databricks page it came from. Databricks updates its policies and exam guides, so confirm anything time-sensitive on the linked page before your appointment.

> Also see the [Databricks GenAI practice questions](https://www.savemycert.com/practice/databricks-generative-ai-engineer-associate/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=databricks-genai-study-guide) and [timed mock exams](https://www.savemycert.com/mocks/databricks-generative-ai-engineer-associate/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=databricks-genai-study-guide).

## Booking the exam

Databricks certification exams are delivered by Kryterion. You register through Databricks' Webassessor site, either from the register link on the exam page or directly in the Webassessor catalog. If you have a voucher, you enter the code on the exam checkout page and then complete checkout.

The registration fee is USD 200, plus applicable taxes required by local law. There is no prerequisite, but Databricks highly recommends related training and about six months of hands-on experience with the tasks in the exam outline. The exam is offered in English, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese and Korean.

Source: [Databricks Certified Generative AI Engineer Associate](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/genai-engineer-associate), [Certification FAQ](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/faq), [Exam guide (PDF)](https://www.databricks.com/sites/default/files/2026-10/databricks-certified-gen-ai-associate-exam-guide-august-2026.pdf)

## Rescheduling and canceling

You reschedule by signing in to your Webassessor account. The FAQ says an online proctored exam must be rescheduled more than 24 hours before the scheduled time, and a test center exam more than 72 hours before. Missing the deadline means a rescheduling fee, and the FAQ does not state the amount. The exam terms and conditions use a single 72-hour notice period for rescheduling or canceling, and say you may be required to pay an additional fee. Because the two pages differ, plan on the 72-hour deadline and check both pages before you change a booking.

Source: [Certification FAQ](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/faq), [Exam Terms and Conditions](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/terms-and-conditions)

## Online proctored vs test center

The exam guide lists the delivery method as online proctored. The exam page lists both online and test center delivery. Check the delivery options shown in Webassessor when you book.

For an online proctored exam, the terms and conditions set these rules:

- **No breaks:** be ready to sit for the whole exam. You cannot take a break or step out of the camera's view.
- **Room:** use a quiet, well-lit room and be able to show it to the proctor in a room scan. Nobody else may be in the testing area.
- **Desk:** remove everything except your computer. Food, drink, headphones and earbuds are not allowed.
- **Your system:** you are responsible for meeting the system requirements. If malware that allows remote access to your computer is detected, the exam is suspended.

The FAQ warns that firewalls on work computers can block the proctoring tools and get an exam suspended. It points to Kryterion's online testing requirements, its system check, and its guides to the pre-check and to proctored-exam behaviour.

Source: [Exam Terms and Conditions](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/terms-and-conditions), [Certification FAQ](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/faq), [Exam guide (PDF)](https://www.databricks.com/sites/default/files/2026-10/databricks-certified-gen-ai-associate-exam-guide-august-2026.pdf)

## What to have ready

- A computer that passes [Kryterion's system check](https://www.kryterion.com/systemcheck/) and meets its [online testing requirements](https://kryterion.my.site.com/support/s/article/Online-Testing-Requirements?language=en_US) for computer, camera and browser. Run the check on the machine you will test on, ideally not a locked-down work computer.
- A quiet, well-lit room with a clear desk that you can show on camera.
- Your Webassessor login and booking details.

The Databricks pages linked here do not list ID requirements. Kryterion's pre-check guide, linked from the FAQ, covers the check-in process, so read it before exam day.

Source: [Certification FAQ](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/faq), [Exam Terms and Conditions](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/terms-and-conditions)

## How the exam is structured

You answer 45 scored multiple-choice and multiple-selection questions in 90 minutes. The exam can also include up to 10 unscored items. They are not identified, they do not affect your score, and the time limit already includes extra time for them. No test aids are allowed. All machine learning code in the exam is in Python, and data manipulation code may be given in SQL.

The exam guide in force was published March 18, 2026, and has six sections: Design Applications (14%), Data Preparation (14%), Application Development (30%), Assembling and Deploying Applications (22%), Governance (8%), and Evaluation and Monitoring (12%). Databricks asks you to check the exam guide on its website two weeks before your exam to make sure you are preparing for the current version.

Source: [Exam guide (PDF)](https://www.databricks.com/sites/default/files/2026-10/databricks-certified-gen-ai-associate-exam-guide-august-2026.pdf), [Databricks Certified Generative AI Engineer Associate](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/genai-engineer-associate)

## Scoring and results

Unofficial results are shown as soon as the exam ends. Databricks does not publish passing scores: it sets them through statistical analysis, and they can change. If you pass, Databricks reviews the proctor recording before it notifies you that your badge has been posted.

Source: [Certification FAQ](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/faq)

## After you pass: credential and recertification

Your digital badge and certificate are issued at [credentials.databricks.com](https://credentials.databricks.com/). Databricks does not mail paper certificates. You can opt in to the Databricks Certified Directory.

The certification is valid for two years from the award date, and the badge expires two years after it is awarded. Databricks notifies you before it expires. To recertify, you take the full current live exam.

Source: [Certification FAQ](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/faq), [Exam guide (PDF)](https://www.databricks.com/sites/default/files/2026-10/databricks-certified-gen-ai-associate-exam-guide-august-2026.pdf)

## If you don't pass

You can register again after 14 days, and every attempt must be 14 days after the previous one. Each attempt costs the full exam price. Databricks does not issue retake vouchers. Databricks does not share information about failed attempts.

Source: [Certification FAQ](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/faq), [Exam Terms and Conditions](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification/terms-and-conditions)

## On exam day: practical tips

- **Budget under two minutes a question.** 90 minutes covers 45 scored questions plus up to 10 unscored ones you cannot tell apart, so plan for 55 questions, about 1.6 minutes each.
- **Read for the constraint.** Most options are real Databricks features. The stated requirement (least privilege, no redeploy, no code change, per-user access, lowest cost once quality ties) usually decides which one fits.
- **Do not discuss the questions afterwards.** The terms treat exam content as confidential, and disclosing it violates them.
